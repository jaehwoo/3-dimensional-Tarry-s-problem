The problem and the manuscript claim

Let Vk = ℝ[x, y, z]≤k / ℝ be the space of real polynomials of total degree at most k, modulo constants, with Lebesgue measure in the nonconstant monomial coefficients. Its dimension is Nk = binom(k + 3, 3) − 1. Write χk = binom(k + 3, 4), where binom denotes a binomial coefficient.

For the representative F with constant coefficient zero, the associated oscillatory integral is

ℐk(F)=∫[0,1]3eiF(x,y,z)dxdydz.

For every integer k ≥ 2 and every 0 < p < ∞, the two papers together claim the following sharp integrability range:

ℐk∈Lp(Vk)⟺p>χk+3=(k+34)+3.

Thus the claimed critical Tarry exponent is p3,kTar = 3 + binom(k + 3, 4), with divergence at the endpoint. This concerns integration over the full real coefficient space. The corresponding discrete mean-value problem counts integer solutions of the Parsell–Vinogradov system.

Statements: Paper I, Theorem 1.1 and Paper II, Theorem 1.1.

The proposed proof approach

  1. Complete affine jets. An affine map u ↦ c + Bu gives the complete nonconstant coefficient vector of F(c + Bu) − F(c). The argument chooses physical affine maps independently of the phase and uses their fixed coefficient coordinates.
  2. Geometric assignment and coefficient volume. Regular configurations are treated by filtered Hermite interpolation and polynomial sampling. Configurations near surfaces use transverse derivative coordinates, retaining the lower coefficients lost on differentiation.
  3. Degree ranges and assembly. Paper I develops the shared estimates and the range k ≥ 14. Paper II treats 2 ≤ k ≤ 13 using those estimates. The supplement records the binary-cubic argument and finite coefficient certificates.

Verification and attribution

The repository records checking through ChatGPT Work mode. This is AI-assisted verification; the proof is yet to be human-verified. Lean formalization and verification are still in progress, and a complete Lean verification of the main theorem is not yet available.

Completed and ongoing formalization records are published separately in Tarry-problem-lean-checkpoint-backup. The presence of compiled modules or historical audit records does not by itself establish a complete formal proof of the manuscripts.

The standalone exact-arithmetic checker for the degree-six fixed-graph normal determinant passed its first public GitHub Actions run on 11 November 2026 (UTC). It checks the 12 block determinant factorizations, the signed full determinant factorization, and the degrees and leading coefficients in the Euclidean remainder sequence over the field with 101 elements against values built into the script. The research index gives the scope and reproduction instructions. The manuscript's geometric arguments and proposed Tarry convergence theorem still await independent proof verification.
