Manuscript abstract

We study Tarry's problem for the three-dimensional Parsell–Vinogradov system, namely the integrability, over the polynomial coefficient space, of the oscillatory integral associated with all nonconstant monomials of total degree at most k. For every k ≥ 14, the sharp range is p > binom(k + 3, 4) + 3. The argument uses complete affine jets, a geometric assignment, and coefficient-volume estimates retaining every nonconstant coefficient. Filtered Hermite interpolation treats the regular configurations, while transverse derivative coordinates treat the configurations near surfaces. We include a binary cubic example. The companion paper treats degrees two through thirteen using the shared estimates proved here.
