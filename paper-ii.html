Manuscript abstract

We complete the sharp integrability theorem for polynomial oscillatory integrals in three variables in degrees 2 ≤ k ≤ 13. The sharp range is p > binom(k + 3, 4) + 3. We use the analytic reduction, geometric assignment and coefficient coordinates of the companion paper. The remaining arguments consist of cubic adaptive localization, finite regular sampling estimates, and surface estimates in degrees four through nine. All coefficient counts retain the complete nonconstant polynomial coefficient space.
